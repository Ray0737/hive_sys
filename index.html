Hive Map

Read first

This is a support and training tool, not an official warning or evacuation authority. Official guidance (DDPM, BMA, school or workplace plan) always wins. Data shown is the last fetched state, never live truth.

Toolbar
  • Nearby: tap the map for the closest hospital, shelter, shop and more
  • Measure: drop points A, B, C, drag to adjust, see each leg and the total
  • My location
  • Search a place (needs network). Press / to focus
  • Copy a link to this exact view
  • Export visible layers as GeoJSON
  • Dark, contrast and satellite maps
Sources and licences

Map data: OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), via Overpass. Tiles: OpenFreeMap, CARTO Dark Matter, Esri World Imagery. Hazard data: BMA open data (flood risk, floodgates), USGS (earthquakes), Air4Thai (PM2.5), RainViewer (radar), AWS Terrain Tiles (low-lying ground). Icons: Lucide (ISC). Font: LINE Seed TH.

Military layers (army, navy, air force, ammunition and ordnance depots) show public OpenStreetMap data only, sorted by the tags and names mappers wrote. Ammunition and ordnance sites are marked as hazards to avoid in an emergency, for relief logistics and restricted-area awareness. Nothing is inferred beyond what is already public.
